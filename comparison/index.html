tree-sitter-scss comparison

P233/tree-sitter-scss is a Tree-sitter parser for SCSS and CSS, written from scratch for scss2-mode. This page parses the same source with it and with the official tree-sitter-grammars/tree-sitter-scss, and the grammar and query used by Zed’s SCSS & SASS extension. Each engine uses the revisions shown below. Pick an example or choose Custom source to paste your own. Parsing runs in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
